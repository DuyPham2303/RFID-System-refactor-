Recommended demo startup order

1. MCU / clock initialization
2. Port_Init(&Port_Config)
3. Pwm_Init(&Pwm_Config)
4. Other BSW initialization
5. RTE / application startup
6. Application calls IoHwAb service

Important distinction:

Port_Init()
    configures PA8 electrically for TIM1_CH1.

Pwm_Init()
    configures TIM1 peripheral/channel.

IoHwAb_SetMotorPwm()
    selects the logical channel and requests a duty cycle.

App
    never passes Port_ConfigType directly to Port_Init().
